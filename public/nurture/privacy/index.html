Nurture

Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 August 2026

Nurture is a plant care reminder app. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where it is stored, and how to delete it. It covers the Nurture mobile app only.

What Nurture collects

Your account details. You sign in with Google. Nurture receives your name, email address, and profile picture from that account. Sign-in is handled by Clerk, our authentication provider; Nurture never sees or stores your Google password.

The plant information you enter. Each plant you add stores its nickname, the species you picked, the room label you chose, the dates you marked it watered, and — for plants not in the built-in catalog — the name and watering interval you typed.

Your reminder preferences. The time of day you chose for reminders, your time zone, whether reminders are switched on, and markers tracking your watering streak.

Photos you choose to add. If you attach a photo to a plant, Nurture makes a smaller copy (up to 1024 pixels on the long edge, saved as JPEG) and uploads only that copy. The original photo stays on your device and is never uploaded.

What Nurture does not collect

  • No analytics or tracking. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising. The app shows no ads, and your information is never sold or shared for advertising.
  • No location data. Nurture never requests or records your location.
  • No contacts, microphone, or audio. Audio recording is explicitly disabled in the app's build.
  • No push notification tokens. Reminders are scheduled on your own device, so no notification identifier is ever sent to a server.

Photo library and camera access are requested only at the moment you choose to add a photo, and only the image you pick is used.

How your photos are protected

Plant photos are kept in private storage. They are not publicly reachable, and no one can list or open them by guessing a web address. When the app needs to show your photo, it requests a temporary signed link that works for up to one hour and is issued only to your signed-in session.

Where your information is stored

Nurture uses two service providers, both acting on our behalf:

  • Clerk — handles Google sign-in and your session.
  • Supabase — hosts the database holding your plants and preferences, and the private storage holding your photos.

Access rules on that database and storage are enforced per account, so your records and photos are readable only by your own signed-in session.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your information is kept until you remove it. You are always in control:

  • Delete an individual plant, or remove a single photo, from that plant's screen in the app.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your plants, watering history, reminder preferences, and uploaded photos.

Account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Children

Nurture is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
